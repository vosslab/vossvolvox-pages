3vee molecular volume tools

Browser-based volume assessment and internal-volume extraction for PDB structures.

Calculations run locally with single-threaded WebAssembly. No coordinate upload server is used.

External volumes

Volume Calculation Calculate structure volume for a selected solvent-probe radius. Volume Range Calculate structure volume across a range of probe radii.

Internal volumes

Channel Finder Find and extract major channels based on their size. Single Channel Extraction Extract a channel near a known x, y, z coordinate. Solvent Extraction Extract all solvent-accessible internal volume from a structure. Exit Tunnel Extraction Extract the polypeptide exit tunnel from a 50S ribosomal subunit.

Volume Calculation

Voxel-based solvent-excluded volume and surface-area calculation for PDB coordinates.

Single-threaded WebAssembly; coordinate data and results remain in this browser.
